### 🌟 About Me
I’m currently pursuing a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science & Engineering. My journey began with a deep curiosity for understanding how technology works, which soon evolved into a strong passion for programming. Along the way, I’ve developed a strong problem-solving mindset through coding contests and practical projects while building clean, responsive interfaces.

I’ve developed real-world projects across diverse domains, from ( [Wharf](https://trywharf.vercel.app) ), an AI-powered documentation platform that streamlines developer workflows, to ( [EcoSage](https://ecosage.netlify.app/) ), an AI sustainability companion serving 350+ users, and ( [ZenerSim](https://zenersim.netlify.app/) ), a virtual lab used by 1.5K+ users to visualize complex electronics concepts. I enjoy participating in Hackathons and Open-Source Communities where I can turn creative ideas into meaningful digital products.
